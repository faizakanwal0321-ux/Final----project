WHY CINETELLA SMARTBITE?

No More Waiting.
Just Smart Ordering.

We designed CineTella SmartBite to make ordering food at AUW faster, easier and more organized.

THE PROBLEM

What's Taking So Long?

Getting food during busy hours can be frustrating for both customers and chefs.

⏰ Long Waiting Time

Students and teachers may have to wait while their food is being prepared.

🍽️ Unorganized Orders

When many people order at the same time, it can become difficult to keep track of every order.

👩‍🍳 Kitchen Confusion

Without a clear order list, chefs may find it difficult to organize which meals need to be prepared first.

📚 Lost Time

Waiting for food can take away valuable time between classes, lectures and other university activities.

OUR SOLUTION

What If Your Food Was Ready When You Arrived?

Meet CineTella SmartBite — a simple digital food ordering system designed to make campus dining faster, clearer and more convenient.
